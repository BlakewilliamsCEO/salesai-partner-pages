The Referral Spot Sheet

A one-page field guide for account managers — what Voice AI does, who it's for, what to listen for in your book of clients, and exactly what to say to flag a referral.
What it is

A Voice AI agent is software that answers and makes phone calls in a natural, human-sounding voice — and actually gets things done on the call. Not a phone tree. It talks like a person, understands what the caller wants, and books the appointment, qualifies the lead, and follows up — 24/7, on every call.

What it handles
Inbound + qualification
Every call answered on the first ring, caller's need understood and qualified live.
Booking & scheduling
Drops the job or meeting straight onto the calendar during the call.
Outbound follow-up
Calls web leads back in seconds, chases no-shows, revives aging leads.
Tier-1 support
Answers FAQs — hours, pricing, status — and routes the rest to a human.
How it works
Connect — point their number (or overflow / after-hours) at the agent.
Configure — it learns their services, pricing, FAQs, booking & qualifying rules.
Converse — natural voice, real back-and-forth, understands intent.
Act — books the calendar, qualifies, logs to CRM, texts confirmation.
Hand off — escalates to a human when it should, with full context.
Who it's for
Revenue is won or lost  on the phone and on inbound leads
Appointment / job-based —  they make money when something gets booked
Spending on ads or lead gen but  leaking at the phone or on slow follow-up
Best-fit verticals: home services (HVAC, roofing, pest, plumbing, electrical), med/dental/aesthetics, legal, real estate & property mgmt, auto, financial & insurance, and any multi-location operator.
Step 1 — Identify

Listen for these in your accounts

If a client has said any of this — or you've seen it — they're a referral. You don't need to diagnose; you just need to flag it.
"We can't keep up with the phones" / front desk is slammed
Calls hitting voicemail after hours, weekends, or at lunch
Hiring, training, or churning through reception or a call center
Slow to call web leads back (a speed-to-lead problem)
Spending on ads / SEO but conversion feels soft
Seasonal call spikes they can't staff for
No-shows piling up, no follow-up on open quotes
Multiple locations handling the phone inconsistently
Step 2 — Say

The 3-line talk track

You're making an introduction, not a pitch. Probe → frame → hand off. Keep it casual.
Probe
"Quick one — when a customer calls and nobody picks up, or a lead fills out your form at 9pm, what actually happens to that?"
Frame
"There's a tool we work with — a voice AI that answers every call and follows up on every lead instantly, in a natural voice, and books it right on the calendar. A lot of [their type of business] are using it to stop leaking revenue at the phone."
Hand off
"Want me to connect you with the team that runs it? No pressure — just a quick look at what it'd catch for you."
One rule: spot it and flag it. If they lean in, send the referral to the SalesAI team — we take it from there.
