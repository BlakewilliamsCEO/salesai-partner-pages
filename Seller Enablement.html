04 / Language rule Match the buyer's frame

Company size changes the word.

The same product, two different vocabularies. Pick the right one in the first two minutes of the call.

Owner answers the phone themselves
$1M–3M
Use "AI voice agent" or call her "Sarah."
These owners don't have a CSR role yet — they relate to a person picking up the phone. Selling them a "CSR" is selling a title they haven't hired for.
Has an existing CSR team
$3M–50M
Use "your AI CSR" freely.
These buyers already think in CSR terms and understand augmentation instantly. Leading with "augment your CSRs" gets the head-nod in the first 30 seconds.
