SalesAi

SalesAi builds voice and messaging agents that answer every inbound call, qualify the caller against your criteria, book the appointment on your calendar, and go back to work the leads your team gave up on. It runs nights, weekends, and holidays, in your brand's voice, without adding headcount.

What EO members get:

  • Three months of voice free — the 5,000 minutes/month tier, waived for your first 90 days. A $2,400 value.
  • A complimentary Moment Audit — a working session that shows you where calls are dropping today and what that's worth annually.
  • SalesAi workshops available through your chapter's learning calendar.

How to take advantage: click below, choose a time, and mention your chapter. The three free months are applied at signup — no code, no negotiation.
