3a. What SalesAI does for an HVAC dealer, specifically

The dealer's business runs on the phone: the service call, the no-cool emergency, the replacement estimate, the maintenance renewal. SalesAI puts an always-on agent on that phone line and on the dealer's text number.

  • Inbound: answers every call in the dealer's name, first ring, 24/7. Identifies the caller, pulls history, qualifies (service vs. replacement vs. maintenance, equipment, urgency), books into the dealer's FSM calendar, texts confirmation. Warm-transfers when a human is needed.
  • Outbound: calls new web/form leads back in under 90 seconds. Follows up on open estimates. Confirms and reminds on booked appointments. Re-engages past customers for tune-ups and maintenance plans.
  • Records: logs every conversation, updates the CRM/FSM, and hands the office a summary instead of a voicemail.
  • Built and run for them: a SalesAI agent engineering expert builds the agent with the dealer's scripts, pricing rules, service area, and calendar, then SalesAI runs and tunes it. The dealer never prompts, integrates, or maintains anything.

3e. How SalesAI works with the dealer's (or distributor's) humans

The agent handles the first touch. The humans handle judgment, relationships, and the job.

  • Division of labor. The agent answers, qualifies, books, confirms, follows up, and logs. Humans dispatch, diagnose, quote complex work, handle upset customers, and close big-ticket replacements. Nothing the agent does removes a decision a human should make.
  • Warm transfer and escalation. The agent recognizes when a human is needed (the caller asks, the issue is out of scope, the customer is upset, a pricing question it isn't allowed to answer) and transfers live during business hours or books a callback outside them. The office sets the rules for what transfers and what books.
  • The office stays in the loop. Every conversation lands as a summary, a booked appointment, or a task in the CRM/FSM. The office manager reviews, not re-does.
  • CSRs move up, not out. With the agent taking first calls and follow-ups, the CSR becomes the dispatcher, the closer on estimates, and the person who handles the exceptions. Customers who report ~90% lower hiring cost reduce future hires, not current staff.
  • Humans train it. The dealer's scripts, service area, pricing rules, and FAQs become the agent's knowledge base. SalesAI's agent engineering expert builds it with the dealer, runs a review after go-live, and tunes on the dealer's call recordings. The dealer says what the agent may and may not say.
  • Owner visibility. Booked jobs, answered calls, transfers, and missed opportunities in one view. The owner sees what the phone was doing while the crew was on the roof.
  • For the distributor's team. Same pattern: agent takes overflow and routine outreach, counter and inside sales keep the dealer relationship. Training sessions (section 5) are how the distributor's people learn to run it and explain it to dealers.

The agent's line: "It takes the first call and the follow-up. Your people take the job and the customer. And you decide what it's allowed to say."
