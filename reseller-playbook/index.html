The universal ICP
Section 05

Before the motion,
know the target.

SalesAi fits one recognizable shape of business — and it crosses verticals. Learn the shape and you'll spot a fit on a first call, and walk away from a deal that'll never land before you spend a minute or a credit on it.

What SalesAi does
A voice AI layer for businesses that live or die on phone-based lead conversion.
→Answers every inbound call
→Qualifies the lead against a defined playbook
→Books or routes the qualified opportunity
→Runs outbound follow-up on stalled leads
→Handles tier-one support
The shape of a fit
High inbound call volume from paid demand meets time-sensitive conversion, a defined qualification playbook, and somewhere to book or route the lead. The vertical changes; that shape doesn't.

Section 05

Boosters & disqualifiers.

Ranking boosters — bigger than standard

Not required for fit — but the signal that a deal is bigger than standard. A prospect stacking several is both a stronger fit and a value-priced deal.

→Time-sensitive conversion — speed-to-lead moves close rate
→24/7 demand — leads arrive after hours and weekends
→Multi-location or multi-market
→Seasonal or event-driven demand spikes
→Rising ad spend over the last 6–12 months
→Existing marketing infra — call tracking, CRM, attribution
Disqualifiers — walk away
Pure B2B enterprise with no inbound call motion
Pure ecommerce with no phone conversion
No system to book or route into — this one hides until you ask. Ask early.

The sales motion · Steps 1–3
Section 06
1
SourceYou own it
You spot a prospect that fits the ICP. You know your market and your network — finding the fit is your call. When it's real enough to work, you bring it into the motion by registering it. Everything downstream keys off that.
2
RegisterYou + SalesAi
Drop the deal in the partner Slack channel. That's the trigger that puts our team behind it — to resource it, protect it as yours, and track it alongside you. Give us enough to act on: company, primary contact, rough size and footprint, and where it stands. A "got a lead" one-liner doesn't move anything; a registered deal does.
3
QualifyYou + SalesAi
Run your own first pass against the ICP — hard filters cleared, no disqualifier tripped. Then we sharpen the rest together at the weekly pipeline review. Clearing qualification before you spend is the discipline that protects your credit pool — pre-sale builds draw credits, so make sure a deal is real first.

The sales motion · Steps 4–6
Section 06
4
DemoYou own it · CX builds
Early on we run demos with you — you drive the relationship, we drive product depth. Once you're demo-certified, you run them solo and pull us in for backup.
The unfair advantage
For any qualified, registered deal, you can put our CX team to work before the deal closes — building the actual voice agents for your prospect. A prospect who hears their own agent handle their own calls is the fastest close in this motion. Use it. (Draws from your service-credit pool.)
5
Scope & QuoteYou price · joint on boosters
You set the price your customer pays. Always. For standard deals, work off the reseller price list and keep your margin. When a deal carries ICP boosters, that's your signal to bring it to joint value-scoping — flag them early.
6
CloseYou own it
You close on your paper. You're the reseller of record — your customer signs with you and you bill them directly. We invoice you, never your customer. From their side, you are the company they bought from.

Delivery
Section 07

Turning a closed deal
into a live account.

CX drives delivery. You stay in the relationship; we stand the customer up. The goal is fast, controlled time-to-value — the customer live and seeing it work, without a messy launch.

What we build
CX configures the platform and builds 3–5 voice agents per customer, against their actual call flows, qualification logic, and booking or routing destination. This is the work that makes the agents theirs, not generic.
How we move together
Partnerships, CX, and you run onboarding as one team. CX owns the technical stand-up; you own context and expectations — the intro, what the customer cares about, what "good" looks like to them.
Reaching live
Delivery ends at go-live — agents deployed and handling real calls. Once an account is live, it moves into ongoing managed service.
After live
Ongoing managed service keeps the customer healthy — tuning and optimization. Covered by the recurring managed-service entitlement; anything beyond it draws from your credit pool.
The hand-off from sale to delivery: the trigger and what gets passed at close — e.g. a closed-deal hand-off in Slack with customer details, scope, and any pre-sale build.

Support & escalation
Section 08

Who handles it,
and how fast.

So you're never guessing, and your customer never feels the seams.

Tier-one — platform & CX
Day-to-day support runs through SalesAi. The agents handle front-line call work; CX handles platform-level questions and issues. You don't carry a support desk.
Your role
You own the relationship, so you're often first to hear when something feels off. Your job isn't to fix the platform — it's to flag it fast and clearly so we can.
Funding
Ongoing support sits inside the recurring managed-service entitlement on each account; work beyond that draws from your credit pool.
Escalation & response
Where partners raise an issue, what's standard vs. urgent, and target response times by severity. To be defined with CX.
The boundary
Platform and delivery issues are ours; the customer relationship is yours. We fix the product; you keep the trust.
